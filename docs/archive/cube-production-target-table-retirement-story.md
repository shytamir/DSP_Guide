# Cube Production Target Table Retirement Story

**Status:** Archived historical record. Roadmap Story 10 was completed and
owner-accepted on 2026-08-09.

## Retire the global cube-production target table

**Class:** Structural change.

As a reader orienting to the practical route, I want the guide entrance to
focus on phase purpose and progression so that a low-utility matrix of future
production rates does not invite premature sizing or memorization.

### Reader problem

The global `Quick reference — Cube production targets` table presents
minimum, comfortable, and later rates for five cube colors before the reader
enters the phase route. Its disclaimer distinguishes targets from
commandments, but the table provides little immediate utility and repeats
figures available in their relevant phase contexts.

### Scope

- Remove the `Quick reference — Cube production targets` heading,
  introductory callout and disclaimer, and rate table from the guide.
- Remove references that exist only to direct readers back to that table.
- Preserve phase-local guidance and directly affected navigation or document
  flow.
- Update only directly affected validation or management documentation.

### Non-goals

- Do not remove, revise, or recalculate cube targets inside phase dashboards,
  detailed phase prose, checklists, or production cards.
- Do not replace the table with a new global dashboard, calculator, or summary.
- Do not change the practical route or phase completion criteria.
- Do not include release work.

### Acceptance

- The global cube-production target heading, explanatory prose, and table no
  longer appear in the guide.
- No dead anchor, navigation entry, or prose reference points to the retired
  table.
- Readers can still obtain relevant production guidance within the affected
  phases.
- The surrounding guide entrance reads coherently after the removal.
- The narrowest directly affected Tier 1 contract validation passes.

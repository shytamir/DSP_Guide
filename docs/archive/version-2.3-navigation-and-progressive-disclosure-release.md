# Version 2.3 Navigation and Progressive-Disclosure Release

**Status:** Completed, owner-authorized, and released on 2026-08-09.

## Scope completed

Version 2.3 reconciles the complete reader-facing work published after version
2.2:

- unified the opening route around BLUE and refined the ILS-to-GREEN research
  handoff;
- presented BLUE through WHITE as the mandatory route while preserving WARP
  and LOGISTICS as directly navigable optional capabilities;
- mapped contextual WARP and LOGISTICS entry cues to observable reader needs;
- added the shared three-stage WARP and ILS rail with stable anchors and no
  saved stage state;
- reorganized the ILS manifest so its overview names stage outcomes while each
  stage owns its exact requirements;
- retired the global cube-production target table and reduced premature
  numeric authority in DYSON and PHOTON orientation;
- constrained prose icons to approved semantic surfaces; and
- condensed the DYSON EM-Rail Ejector map while retaining every raw-to-output
  transformation.

The completed twelve-story sequence and its supporting records are preserved
in [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md).

## Product impact

This is a minor structural release. It changes navigation, optional-capability
classification, expedition resumption, and information hierarchy without
changing the practical BLUE-to-WHITE route or the guide's deployment model.

The release preserves:

- 19 phase-relevant production cards and two reusable production references;
- public phase anchors, accepted compatibility anchors, and checklist storage
  keys;
- useful guide prose with every production card collapsed;
- runtime-derived technology and recipe data; and
- the framework-free static site, local-only reader state, and desktop-first
  presentation policy.

## Deferred work

The inactive one-screen DYSON checklist baseline correction was explicitly
deferred by the owner. It is not part of version 2.3 and remains a standing
reference rather than pending implementation.

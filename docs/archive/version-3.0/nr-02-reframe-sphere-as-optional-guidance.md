# NR-02 — Reframe SPHERE as optional guidance

**Status:** Completed and owner-accepted on 2026-08-27. Archived historical
record; this file no longer defines active work.

**Dependency group:** A — Route foundation
**Assessed workload:** Medium
**Class:** Editorial refinement

## Reader need

As a reader choosing permanent construction, I want useful SPHERE guidance without a second progression gate or a duplicate route into PHOTON.

## Authoritative evidence

NR-01 supplies the optional route structure. The approved roadmap assigns Receiver setup and Antimatter progression to one later canonical bridge.

## Intended outcome

NR-02 rewrote SPHERE as self-contained optional guidance and retired its former
phase-gate behavior and sizing targets.

## In-scope surfaces

- SPHERE Goal, overview, instructions, and return-to-route language
- SPHERE completion and checklist surfaces
- SPHERE production shapes and Editor guidance
- DYSON's single optional-path handoff

## Approved specification

- Present DYSON as the prescribed orbital-power route. DYSON may link once
  to SPHERE with a concise permanence tradeoff.
- Replace SPHERE phase-gate language with a short optional-path overview:
  availability after GREEN, permanence tradeoff, route-specific production,
  basic Editor workflow, and operating conditions worth watching.
- Remove **Move on when**, **Ready to move on when**, completion checklists, and
  equivalent requirement language.
- Add a neutral **Return to the route** link to the canonical Receiver and
  Antimatter bridge without requiring a Sphere target.
- Remove Receiver setup, Antimatter conversion, and PHOTON gating from SPHERE.
- Preserve useful production shapes and Editor guidance.
- Remove exact throughput, launch-count, and Dyson-power figures used only to
  size the former PHOTON gate. Do not replace them with a new target.

## Non-goals and preserved contracts

- Do not define a SPHERE completion condition, capstone, mandatory build size, preferred design, or power target.
- Do not create a comprehensive Editor tutorial, blueprint, calculator, or strategy comparison.
- Do not duplicate Receiver or PHOTON instructions.
- NR-03 owns the separate technology and research cleanup.

## Gates satisfied

- Technical validation established contract and presentation consistency.
- The owner accepted the deployed reader-facing result on 2026-08-27.

- It retains enough guidance to begin permanent construction.
- It contains no completion gate, Receiver procedure, Antimatter gate, or PHOTON-sized exact figures.
- The prescribed route returns through one canonical Receiver bridge.

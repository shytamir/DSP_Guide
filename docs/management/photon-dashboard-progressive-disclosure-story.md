# PHOTON Dashboard Progressive-Disclosure Story

**Status:** Under implementation as Roadmap Story 11 after Roadmap Story 10
owner acceptance.

## Describe the PHOTON state without prescribing exact figures

**Class:** Editorial refinement.

As a reader entering PHOTON while Dyson output is still growing, I want the
phase dashboard to describe the state I am pursuing so that a fully supplied
reference build does not appear to be an entry requirement or fixed gate.

### Reader problem

The PHOTON dashboard currently combines immediate orientation with an exact
Receiver count, the fully supplied Critical Photon and Antimatter rates, and
an exact Antimatter stockpile. Those figures are explained in the detailed
reference layer, but their dashboard placement makes the desired state read
like a set of instructions.

### Scope

- Remove exact counts, rates, capacities, and stockpile figures from the
  PHOTON phase dashboard.
- Reduce the affected dashboard language to concise descriptions of the
  desired operating and transition states.
- Preserve the dashboard's research direction and observable warnings.
- Keep accurate figures in the existing PHOTON detailed reference sections.
- Update only directly affected validation or management documentation.

### Non-goals

- Do not remove or recalculate PHOTON figures outside the dashboard.
- Do not change the Dyson supply model, Receiver behavior, Antimatter recipe,
  production cards, or WHITE requirements.
- Do not turn the dashboard into a procedure or add a new disclosure control.
- Do not perform a document-wide dashboard rewrite.
- Do not include release work.

### Acceptance

- The PHOTON dashboard contains no exact Receiver count, production rate,
  capacity, or Antimatter stockpile target.
- Its main-goal, desired-state, and transition language is concise and
  descriptive rather than instructional.
- The dashboard still tells a returning reader what PHOTON is establishing,
  what to watch, and what observable state supports moving into WHITE.
- Existing detailed PHOTON reference figures remain available and accurate.
- The narrowest directly affected Tier 1 contract validation passes.

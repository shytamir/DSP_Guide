# BOOTSTRAP and BLUE Unification

## Story 1 — Structural consolidation

**Status:** Implemented and reviewed.

As a new player, I want the mall and blue science presented as one opening
BLUE phase so that the guide reflects their overlapping construction without
making me navigate two artificial phases.

### Scope

- Make BLUE the sole opening phase and retain its current phase number, `1`.
- Place the former BOOTSTRAP material before blue-science automation within
  that phase.
- Preserve `#blue` as the canonical phase anchor.
- Preserve `#bootstrap` as a compatibility anchor at the mall subsection.
- Remove BOOTSTRAP from the phase rail and Quick Progress Index.
- Combine the BOOTSTRAP and BLUE entries in the one-screen checklist.
- Preserve all existing card IDs and the complete 18-card inventory.
- Keep the mall and blue-science card controls independently scoped.
- Preserve saved checklist identity for relocated BOOTSTRAP objectives.
- Update only directly affected validation, navigation, and documentation.
- Leave production values, card contents, and substantive phase prose for the
  editorial story.

### Definition of Done

- The guide contains 12 phase sections and exactly 18 phase cards.
- BLUE contains distinct mall and blue-science subsections.
- All later phase numbers remain unchanged.
- `#blue` and legacy `#bootstrap` links both resolve.
- The phase rail and Quick Progress Index expose one opening BLUE entry.
- Both opening card-control groups affect only their own cards.
- Existing opening checklist progress survives the structural move.
- Internal anchors, technology references, card links, and interactions remain
  valid.
- Deployment, card-system, and checklist validation pass.
- No substantive editorial rewrite is included.

## Story 2 — Editorial unification

**Status:** Implemented.

As a new player, I want the opening BLUE phase to explain how the mall and
first science line grow together so that I know blue cubes alone do not mean I
am ready to leave the phase.

### Scope

- Rewrite only the combined BLUE phase's dashboard, Goal, Research first, How
  much is enough, Watch for, completion checklist, and Next text.
- Establish the opening rhythm: unlock the basic factory actions; take useful
  no-matrix technologies and upgrades while mall buffers fill; automate blue
  science; use blue research to finish gated mall branches; stabilize power.
- Bring Steel Smelting, Reclamation, and the simple Foundation buffer naturally
  into the combined phase.
- State the expected mature opening load of approximately 15–20 MW.
- Recommend eight Thermal Power Plants alongside the existing wind network.
- Encourage continued Wind Turbine expansion as protection against coal
  interruptions.
- Keep Foundation as prose rather than restoring a card.
- End with RED as the single next progression phase.
- Do not alter production-card contents or established output figures.

### Definition of Done

- The phase never implies that automated blue cubes alone complete BLUE.
- Research advice distinguishes no-matrix opportunities from blue-funded
  research.
- Mall work and science work read as parallel parts of one opening phase.
- Power guidance is concrete and appears where the reader first needs it.
- The completion checklist requires both dependable blue research and freedom
  from most repetitive handcrafting.
- Former BOOTSTRAP and BLUE instructions are not duplicated.
- The prose remains friendly, decisive, concise, and beginner-facing.
- RED receives a clean handoff without inheriting opening-phase cleanup.

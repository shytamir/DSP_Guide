# Changelog

## 2.3 — 2026-08-09

Version 2.3 is a navigation and progressive-disclosure release. It clarifies
the mandatory route, makes optional capabilities easier to enter at the right
time, and gives the two expedition-shaped sections a reusable stage-resumption
model.

### Added

- Added a projecting `I / II / III` stage rail for WARP and ILS with stable,
  directly addressable stage anchors and no stored stage progress.
- Added contextual WARP entry destinations for early preparation and later
  automated import.
- Added durable validation for route identity, optional-capability separation,
  expedition anchors, icon-placement boundaries, and accepted card structure.

### Changed

- Presented BLUE through WHITE as the ten-step mandatory route while keeping
  WARP and LOGISTICS directly navigable as optional capabilities.
- Aligned LOGISTICS entry guidance with repeated expansion rather than mere
  technical availability.
- Reworked the ILS-to-GREEN research handoff and reorganized the ILS manifest
  around stage outcomes while preserving exact requirements where actionable.
- Unified the opening route around BLUE and shifted DYSON and PHOTON guidance
  toward observable factory behavior rather than premature numeric gates.
- Restricted prose icons to approved semantic surfaces and condensed the
  DYSON EM-Rail Ejector production map without losing raw-supply provenance.

### Removed

- Retired the low-utility global cube-production target table; phase-local
  production guidance remains available where readers need it.

### Preserved

- The BLUE-to-WHITE practical route, 19 production cards, two reusable
  production references, public phase anchors, and checklist storage keys.
- The framework-free static architecture, local-only reader state, and
  runtime-derived game-data boundaries.
- The desktop-first presentation policy; no mobile-polish expansion is part of
  this release.

### Deferred

- The inactive one-screen DYSON checklist baseline correction remains outside
  this release by owner decision.

## 2.2 — 2026-08-08

Version 2.2 is a maintenance release focused on presentation quality and
targeted guide refinement. It does not substantially change the guide's
structure, progression order, phase objectives, card inventory, or local-only
interaction model.

### Changed

- Added a restrained space-themed background with a dedicated 4K source for
  high-resolution displays.
- Improved high-resolution presentation by doubling the phase navigation rail
  above the 1080p display threshold.
- Aligned reading-pane text, boxes, tables, and cards to a consistent right
  edge while preserving their left indentation, and centered both guide logos.
- Refined the reusable Electromagnetic Turbine production map and reconciled
  the first-ILS finite material bill and power-spike guidance.
- Added and verified the repository-local agent bootstrap used for repeatable
  validation and rendered browser checks.

### Unchanged

- The 12-section progression and optional-route structure.
- The 19 phase cards and two reusable production references.
- Runtime-derived guide data, checklist storage contracts, and static-only
  deployment architecture.

Earlier release history remains available in Git and in `docs/archive/`.
